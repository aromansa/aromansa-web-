Quick FAQs

Common questions about essential oils and Aromansa products

1

How do I use essential oils safely?

Always dilute essential oils with a carrier oil (like coconut or jojoba oil) before applying to skin. Perform a patch test first to check for sensitivity. Avoid direct sunlight after using citrus oils, as they can cause photosensitivity. Keep out of reach of children and never ingest unless under professional guidance.

2

What's the difference between therapeutic grade and regular?

Therapeutic grade essential oils are pure, unadulterated extracts that have been tested for potency and quality. They contain no synthetic additives or fillers. Regular-grade oils may be diluted or contain synthetic compounds, making them less effective for aromatherapy and wellness purposes. Aromansa only offers therapeutic grade oils.

3

Can I use essential oils for my children?

Yes, but with caution. Always use kid-safe oils like lavender, chamomile, or sweet orange in highly diluted forms. Avoid strong oils like peppermint and eucalyptus for children under 6. We recommend consulting a pediatric aromatherapist and always diffusing in well-ventilated areas with shorter exposure times for kids.

4

How should I store essential oils?

Store essential oils in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep bottles tightly closed to prevent oxidation. Most oils last 2-3 years when stored properly, though citrus oils have a shorter shelf life of about 1-2 years. Original dark glass bottles are designed to protect the oils from UV damage.
